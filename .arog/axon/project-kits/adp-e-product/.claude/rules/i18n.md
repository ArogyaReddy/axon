# i18n (adp-e-product)

- Every `translate(key)` needs the key in both `messages-en.json` and `messages-es.json`:
  controllers `i18n/controllers/<noun.verb>/`, events `i18n/event/<noun.verb>/`. A missing key does not throw: the raw
  key shows in production UI.
- Change English and Spanish in the same change, with a real Spanish translation.
- Checked by `check-i18n` (run by `axon-verify`) and the `post-edit-check` hook.
